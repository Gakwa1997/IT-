Questions

What schools usually ask us

We already have someone who handles our computers. Why would we need you?

Most schools have someone capable who fixes machines when they break. That is repair, not IT management. We handle the parts that rarely get done: verified backups, security and access reviews, licence and cost optimisation, documentation, and forward planning. In many cases we work alongside your existing technician rather than replacing them — and we train them.

Is the free IT audit genuinely free?

Yes. We visit, assess every system, and give you a written report with findings and recommendations. You keep the report whether or not you engage us. We do this because in our experience schools underestimate their exposure — particularly around backups — and the report makes the decision obvious either way.

What if we're outside Kigali?

We serve schools upcountry with a blended model: most work is handled through remote assistance, with scheduled on-site visits at agreed intervals. Travel is agreed transparently and separately. Several of the most valuable things we do — account administration, backups, website and domain work, security reviews — do not require us to be physically present.

Do we own our systems, or are we locked into you?

You own everything. Domains are registered in the school's name, admin credentials are held by the school, and every system we build is documented and handed over. Contracts are monthly with 30 days' notice. We would rather keep clients because the service is good than because leaving is painful.

Can you help us attract more students online?

Yes — this is part of the consultancy layer. A professional website, correct Google Business and Maps listing, working enquiry forms, search visibility and an active social presence measurably affect enrolment. For schools seeking international students or partnerships, we also prepare the digital presentation that those audiences expect.

How quickly do you respond when something breaks?

Within four hours during business hours for all plans, with priority handling on Managed School and Campus Partner. Critical failures affecting teaching — internet down, email down, exam systems — are treated as urgent and escalated immediately, remotely first and on site if needed.
